# Review discoveries and enrichment candidates

Use when the user asks to audit an answer, reconcile conflicting evidence, or preserve a useful finding. Freeze the baseline question, identities, sources and claims. Compare each new claim as already known, novel and source-verified, plausible/unverified, generic advice or unsupported. A model's agreement is not corroboration.

Inspect original sources for additions that affect the decision. Record the exact disagreement: identity, time period, payment direction, aggregation, purpose, program scope, geography, instrument or current access. Keep the old observation and the correction's source legible; do not silently overwrite history. Report which conclusion changes and which decisions remain valid.

When researching a whole organization, preserve its dominant identity and catalog its relevant embedded programs separately. A finding about one population does not reclassify the entire parent. Capture affiliates, member networks, referrals, implementation partners and funders as typed, source-bearing proposed relationships.

The useful output is a reviewed answer plus a small verification queue: claim, subject/object identity, source and locator, checked date, confidence, graph relevance, and next action. When the user asks to save it, follow [connection boundaries](connection-boundaries.md). Otherwise retain a conversation review note. Ingestion, classification refresh, embeddings, graph promotion and materialization remain internal operator jobs; do not claim they ran.
