## Work to complete

1. Interpret the strongest available organization and peer evidence.
2. Build a persuasive case that follows from the established strategy.
3. Identify five to ten plausible funders that follow from the case, peer
   patterns, geography, intended uses, and known relationships.
4. Write substantive, copy-ready prose for Chapters 4–6.
5. Record confidence, caveats, and validation needs only in the private
   validation ledger.

The chapters continue the existing argument:

- Evidence Base interprets organization and peer evidence in 225–325 words.
- Case Design builds the case from Chapters 1–4 in 300–450 words.
- Opportunities connects funding routes and funders to the case and peer
  patterns in 300–450 words.

Each chapter should normally contain two to four developed paragraphs. Write
direct organizational prose. Do not expose confidence labels, research tasks,
readiness commentary, consultant recommendations, or a limitations memo.

Build the funder map in this order:

1. Known current or previous funders of the focal organization.
2. Funders connected to selected peers in the supplied context.
3. Funders associated with the same program, population, geography, or
   strategic lane.
4. Additional model-suggested funders that plausibly fit the completed case.

Known current funders are existing or stewardship relationships, not cold
prospects. A peer-funder relationship may be labeled documented only when the
supplied context supports it; otherwise use shared strategy fit. Never invent
