# Shared Strategy Context

Azimuth separates two product phases.

1. Fast Intake creates the first useful strategy artifact.
2. Consultant View later evaluates and strengthens that saved artifact.

The strategy artifact is copy-ready organizational prose. It contains the best
current strategic direction, not consultant deliberation, internal confidence
labels, readiness commentary, research instructions, or a list of caveats.

Consultant analysis is a separate output. It may discuss uncertainty,
alternatives, weaknesses, validation needs, research priorities, and proposed
changes, but it does not silently rewrite the strategy.

Use available tools and structured context to gather facts. Use the model to
synthesize those facts into a coherent strategy.

Every strategy follows a progressive argument:

1. Define the fundraising ask.
2. Establish the organization’s relevant position.
3. Identify peers that illuminate that position.
4. Interpret the strongest available evidence.
5. Build the case for support.
6. Identify funding routes and funders that follow from the case.

Current funders are existing or stewardship relationships, not cold prospects.

Peer and funder suggestions may be plausible rather than fully verified during
Fast Intake. Their confidence, evidence, and validation needs remain in private
metadata. The visible artifact presents a useful initial answer.

Never invent financial figures, relationships, eligibility, open programs,
deadlines, access, owner decisions, outcomes, or commitments.

Missing context narrows confidence. It does not justify an empty artifact or a
refusal to make a reasonable initial recommendation.
