# Route balance and operating roadmap

Translate reviewed strategy into a realistic fundraising plan. Use only
accepted route decisions, qualified priorities, current and former supporters,
verified access evidence, restrictions, current use of funds, organizational
capacity and calendar, risk tolerance, and the selected strategic angle.

## When the stage is `funding_portfolio`

Recommend a coherent route and target balance across renewals and new money,
applications and relationship-building, near-term openings and longer bets,
and the applicable unrestricted, program, public, corporate/bank, individual,
earned, capital, or in-kind routes. Explain each route's job, evidence basis,
time horizon, effort, access, restrictions, risk, and capacity demand.

Set a conservative multi-year ramp and per-channel solicitation and dollar
goals only when their basis is supplied. Use the peer benchmark as a
destination or diagnostic reference, not an automatic annual target. Make the
tradeoffs explicit, recommend a sequence, and preserve excluded or deferred
routes with reasons. Do not assume banks, RFPs, or any other channel deserve
priority without organization-specific evidence and an accepted decision.

## When the stage is `operating_system`

Convert the approved balance into an owned 8–12 week plan and ongoing
management cadence. For every priority account or workstream record:

- route, rationale, and current strategic disposition;
- evidence and relationship status as separate fields;
- owner and supporting role;
- next validation or conversation and desired outcome;
- proposed ask or learning goal and its basis;
- due date, wave, and dependencies;
- required case, budget, approval, attachment, or other material;
- next pipeline stage and review date; and
- stewardship or follow-up responsibility.

Sequence early learning calls, introductions, readiness materials, asks, and
decisions. Define a lightweight opportunity tracker, review cadence,
acknowledgment/stewardship rhythm, staged materials plan, and refresh trigger.
Do not invent owners or dates: retrieve the current calendar and role context,
then ask for only the unresolved assignments the human must make.

Advice remains a proposal until adopted. A named person is not a verified
contact, and a public connection is not a warm introduction. If qualification
is incomplete, return the affected items to the validation queue rather than
placing them in an execution wave.

Return typed route-balance, roadmap, and account-plan proposals plus only the
authorized section patches, or `no_change` when the accepted plan remains
current. Represent the methodology through route/target balance and account
plans; do not create or revive the scrapped Portfolio UI.
