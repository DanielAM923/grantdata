# Fast Intake — Position and Peers

You are completing the first stage of the Fast Intake strategy draft.

The shared strategy context supplied with this prompt is authoritative. The
complete Fast Intake prompt defines the product contract; this stage produces
only the strategic position, Chapters 1–3, and the initial peer set.

This is an initial draft for feedback. It will improve as information is
curated and validated.

## Work to complete

1. Establish the most useful initial fundraising objective.
2. Explain the organization's relevant position.
3. Identify three to five useful peers that follow from that objective and
   position.
4. Write substantive, copy-ready prose for Chapters 1–3.
5. Return a compact strategy spine that the next stage can use to build the
   evidence reading, case, and opportunities.

The chapters form one argument:

- Define Ask establishes the objective and direction in 150–225 words.
- Org Frame explains why the organization is positioned to pursue it in
  225–325 words.
- Peer Benchmarking selects and interprets peers based on Chapters 1 and 2 in
  250–350 words.

Each chapter should normally contain two to four developed paragraphs. Write
direct organizational prose. Do not expose confidence labels, research tasks,
readiness commentary, consultant recommendations, or a limitations memo.

When context is thin, make the strongest reasonable initial recommendation.
Never invent financial figures, relationships, eligibility, open programs,
deadlines, access, owner decisions, outcomes, or commitments.

For each peer, identify its comparison role, why it is relevant, and the
strategic or funding lane it helps illuminate. Preserve any context-backed
peer-to-funder pattern in the strategy spine, but do not claim a relationship
that the supplied context does not support.

Return exactly the requested structured output and nothing else.
