# Goal calibration

Use when recommending a fundraising goal, channel portfolio, cash forecast, or staffing ramp. Keep the recommendation useful while making its construction inspectable.

## Four different numbers

- **Peer ambition:** a historical ratio/scale sensitivity, with cohort/year/scope caveats. It describes a possible destination, not attainable first-year receipts.
- **Solicitation plan:** count of accessible, qualified asks × proposed ask size. It measures requests, not awards.
- **Commitments:** full award/pledge face value and term, recorded once; separately label conditional or prospective awards.
- **Cash in the planning horizon:** payments actually due within the horizon, plus distinct new-cash scenarios. A three-year commitment is not three years of cash available now. Restricted cash is not unrestricted spending capacity.

Never use “cash/commitment” or “committed/received” as one metric. Unknown renewals/access mean unknown, not a confirmed zero baseline. Show verified receipts/scheduled awards separately from prospective renewals and new asks; avoid counting an existing award as both a renewal and new money.

## Construct the first-year recommendation

Start from confirmed staff hours, reachable accounts, a costed funding need and award/payment timing. Show a short table: channel, qualified asks, ask size, total solicited, assumed award conversion, in-horizon payment fraction, prospective cash, and access/capacity condition. Where the user supplies planning assumptions, calculate with those assumptions. For a simple homogeneous channel: prospective cash = ask count × ask amount × assumed award rate × fraction paid within the horizon. For varying awards, calculate by account/payment schedule. This is a planning scenario, not evidence of probability or an approved forecast.

If renewals, access and staff time are unknown, recommend a bounded first phase with a role/hour proposal, learning milestones and a conditional scenario. Do not recommend an unsupported numeric first-year target, including a provisional planning goal. Merely labeling a guessed increment provisional does not supply its construction. If no accessible ask plan can yet be supported, recommend the initial phase and a date for adopting a target. If an illustrative scenario helps, show every assumed ask, conversion and payment input and label it an illustration awaiting access/capacity confirmation; do not carry historical contributions forward as its renewal base. Do not label an unsupported numeric case a floor, minimum, conservative forecast, or attainable target. A downside scenario can still produce no new cash; show that when no awards are secured. Do not invent a universal 0.5/1.0 FTE threshold, 3× pipeline rule, typical check size or success rate. If useful assumptions are proposed, label them, explain the reason and show what changes if they fail. Do not prescribe a hire from a peer ratio.

A board-selected aspirational target can remain visible even when the scenario falls short. Show the gap and the concrete extra access, asks, timing, staffing or cost reductions needed. On new evidence, recompute the affected cash/commitment totals and workload; do not merely rename the old target “stretch.”

## Before delivering

Check that channel sums match, commitments and cash are separate, payment dates fall in the stated horizon, future years are excluded, and source/assumption/decision status is visible. Distinguish total cash, incremental new cash, and renewals. Round only after calculating. Report the recommendation and next leadership decision plainly; detailed arithmetic can sit in a compact table.
