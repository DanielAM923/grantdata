# Public routes, donor evidence and capital

Read only the part relevant to the user's funding lane.

## Public bridge routes

Reconcile user-stated current/lapsed supporters with observed sources before calling a funder a net-new gap. Incomplete inbound coverage means incomplete exclusions. Scope close-peer overlap by actual operating market; distinguish address from service area. Inspect public boards/trustees, fellowship cohorts, co-panels, project partners and governance pages for a possible institutional path. Cite the connection and date; the user must verify personal warmth or access. An open application does not require inventing a bridge.

Separate direct grantmakers, community foundations, DAF sponsors, regrantors, operating partners, corporate sponsors, public agencies and unknown roles. DAFs are advised-donor clues, not ordinary apply-to institutions. A suggested bridge is a next research/conversation question, not an authorized contact or promised introduction.

## Public donor honor rolls

Find official peer annual/impact reports, donor lists, gala programs, supporter pages and public advised-fund references using native research/document reading. Extract only names actually in donor/supporter context; exclude headers, menus and event attendees presented without donation evidence. Keep the source, giving tier/year if stated, short passage and identity uncertainty. Separate confirmed public supporter observations from sponsor/event context and unresolved DAF clues.

A roster appearance is not evidence of wealth, present willingness, reachable contact or warm access. Public profile clues require disambiguation; no private donor list or private social-network access is supplied by this plugin. Output a reviewable lead queue, not outreach-ready instructions. Do not contact anyone without the user's authorization.

## Housing and other non-grant capital

Inspect developer/project pages, lender/CDFI announcements, syndicator deals, public-finance awards and official bank programs. Record institution, role, project, date, instrument, amount where supported, primary source and confidence. Keep construction debt, LIHTC equity, guarantees, subsidies, public contracts, bank philanthropy and services grants in distinct lanes. Existing bank relationships and CRA frameworks do not guarantee a gift.

Use archetypes as search leads appropriate to the domain: banks, CDFIs, syndicators, AHP/public channels and supportive-housing intermediaries for housing; actual payer/contract models for other sectors. A single deal does not establish a general funding mandate. Match public programs to this applicant and market before recommending a route. No typed capital-mapper pipeline or automatic graph write runs here.
