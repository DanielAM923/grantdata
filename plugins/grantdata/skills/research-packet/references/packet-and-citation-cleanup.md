# Packet and citation cleanup

Select the audience's decision and strongest reviewed evidence. Keep a compact main brief and a source/verification appendix when helpful. Do not bury a caveat that changes the conclusion. Preserve low-confidence leads in a clearly labeled queue rather than polishing them into recommendations.

Clean extracted titles and passages of navigation, login/cookie text, HTML entities and raw peer-list/table leakage. Give each case a readable summary, relevance, exact source and evidence limitation. Use a quote only when the original passage was inspected; otherwise paraphrase accurately. Name the public report/program/filing in reader-facing citations; an internal table can be traceability but is not the sole authority for a public claim.

Charts and tables should remain intelligible when copied: metric, units, cohort/scope, actual year, source and material caveat belong in the visual. Funding not assessed differs from funding searched but not found. Grant, public funding, capital and intermediary roles stay labeled.

For an integrated strategy memo adapt this structure: executive recommendation/decision; organization position and comparable benchmark; chosen case/routes/markets and goals with basis; qualified accounts and access/readiness; first-weeks roadmap with owners/timing; longer program/evidence bets; systems, responsibilities, sources and refresh triggers. Do not reuse a previous client's amounts, named owners or warm paths as defaults. Use honest draft/decision/roadmap maturity and make required decisions clear.
